# Piezoelectric MEMS Cooler for Confined Spaces

Frore Systems MEMS air cooler but even smaller. 9 mm x 9 mm x 1.7 mm, consumes 11 mW and removes 500 mW of heat flow rate.
   - the backflow suppression is fluidic: "During the suction phase, the upward motion of the actuator generates negative pressure inside the cavities. By this time, the previously ejected high-speed jet puffs have arrived around the outlet regions. Their position away from the jet orifices, combined with their downward momentum, prevents them from being drawn back."
