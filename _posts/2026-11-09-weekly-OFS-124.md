# Wafer Level Test and Cowboy Acid Etch

this bare wafer sitting on optical breadboard "wafer level test setup" really shows how different industry or even same industry different era folks do things very differently.
   - here is another one: before LN became TFLN, bulk LN folks would scribe the chip/wafer name using a diamond scriber on the backside (now people do proper litho for labels and names), and when they inspect poling domains with HF etch (etch speed is different along crystal +z and -z), they put the wafer on a piece of paper, pour HF, and let its surface tension hold itself within the wafer.. maybe not everyone at the time was doing this cowboy style acid etch.

# Labyrinth Filter and 287 GHz Link

when you make a labyrinth on your chip, some of the waves will get addicted to certain sections of it, some of them give up finding the exit and head back out from the entrance, some of them eventually get lost, and thats how you get a filter.
![20261108_042352_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20261021_20261109/20261108_042352_0.jpg)
   - it is a dual polarization wireless comm at 287 GHz with 400 Gbps and 16-QAM. Funny the modulation is easier done at telecom C band, and then "shifted" to 287 GHz with optical heterodyne, including the polarizations.
   - Dittmer2026: [414.7-Gbit/s Single-Aperture Dual-Polarization 287 GHz Wireless Link](https://doi.org/10.1109/TTHZ.2026.3749414)
