# Fiber Alignment with Microlens Ring

how do you align a free space mode into a single mode fiber? Power out of the SMF only gives you a single number, so you need more to feedback the tip and tilt. A quadrant photodiode is a good option, but now you have one more thing to align in your system.
   - These telescope people did it by surrounding the SMF980 with six 105 um core size MMFs, plus a 3d printed microlens-ring to minimize loss into the gap between the fiber cores. Misalignment would appear as higher power from the MMFs, and they got 6 numbers to potentially feedback on more stuff.
   - The back illuminated image is a nice comparison between the core sizes lol.
![20260728_154242_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20250512_20260803/20260728_154242_0.jpg)
![20260728_154242_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20250512_20260803/20260728_154242_1.jpg)
![20260728_154242_2.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20250512_20260803/20260728_154242_2.jpg)
   - the earliest version had seven lens for an all-MMF array, and they removed the center one for the SMF. They've been running it in Large Binocular Telescope (LBT) since 2019, the lens is still attached fine, and is the longest telescope deployment of a two-photon-polymerized optical component.
   - Dietrich2017: [Printed freeform lens arrays on multi-core fibers for highly efficient coupling in astrophotonic systems](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.25.018288)
   - Hottinger2019: [Focal Plane Tip-Tilt Sensing for Improved Single-Mode Fiber Coupling using a 3D-printed Microlens-Ring](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.09537)
   - Hottinger2021: [On-sky results for the novel integrated micro-lens ring tip-tilt sensor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.10152)
   - Harris2026: [Long-term performance of the MLR-TT sensor: two-photon polymerization validation in the telescope environment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20244)
