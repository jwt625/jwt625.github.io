# AstroPIC Integrated Photonic Coronagraph

TIL people are trying to do coronagraph for exoplanet discovery with a PIC (AstroPIC!) because you can do arbitrary spatial mode mapping with grating couplers and a MZI mesh, hence you can implement the occulting disk on the chip. It's also an OPA, so maybe Taara can repurpose their OPA chip for exoplanet discovery lol.
![20261023_023615_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20260516_20261027/20261023_023615_0.jpg)
   - they've made cascaded MZI with really impressive extinction ratio... not many people have 90+ dB dynamic range optical characterization setup other than an OSA.

# Thin-Film Lithium Tantalate WDM Modulators

im getting the vibe that they are trying to shove this into nature photonics.. anyways ~100 GHz channel spacing WDM thin-film lithium tantalate modulator chip from EPFL. You can dream and you can tell stories but they are unlikely to go into CPO. Both TFLN and TFLT do not win over footprint, they win over IL and bandwidth, and the crap AWG mux/demux just killed that advantage. It will get into pluggables and maybe NPO as well if you fold the electrodes. Monolithic TFLN pluggable probably starting early 2028, the chip itself for 3.2T 400GPL DR8 is almost trivial to make. Heterogeneous TFLN on SiPho maybe another 2-3 years.
