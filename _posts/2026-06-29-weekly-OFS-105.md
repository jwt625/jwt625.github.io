# Ultrasound phased array and CMUT on ASIC

   - the arrays are synchronized but not to the coherent degree yet, but nothing stopping the whole thing from being coherent soon, which means it can revert the measured scattering, and create all kinds of fields like acoustic tweezers in your body and e.g. move stuff around lol

# 1060nm VCSEL single-mode fiber link

people usually use multimode fiber (MMF, OM3 and OM4 etc) with ~850 nm VCSELs. At 1060 nm you need custom MMF, or a ~1 um wavelength single mode fiber (SMF-28 for 1.5 um is multimode here).
   - guess what the insertion loss is between the 1060 nm VCSEL and the SMF?
![20260623_152844_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20241104_20260628/20260623_152844_0.jpg)
   - about 1 dB, pretty damn decent, probably better than COUPE's grating couplers. They sent 8x100G PAM4 over 2 km with this VCSEL + SMF. From Furukawa and FUJIFILM.
   - Yoshida2026: [A 106-Gb/s × 8-Channel 1060-nm Single-Mode VCSEL-Based Ultra-Compact CPO Transceiver enabling 2-km Parallel-Optical Links]( https://doi.org/10.1109/ECTC51846.2026.00020)

# Planar integrated free-space optics module

I wish people read old works more. Look at this wild "chip" from 20 years ago, to be accurate it is an assembled 3D optical multilayer module.
   - They also called it Planar-integrated free-space optics (PIFSO)... It has multiple layers with embedded gratings and microlens, and got photodiodes and laser diodes on the two surfaces. Light would bounce around inside and go in and out of PCB integrated waveguides. Yeah you read it right, it was during the time where people were into optoelectronic PCB with polymer optical waveguides, so you get to see them working together!
   - It was funded by EU's High-speed OptoeLectronic Memory Systems (HOLMS) project, to run a JEPG2000
   - decoder on an optical processor-memory bus in a multiprocessor system lol.
   - one version of the future optical interconnects are gonna be built by AR glasses people, they dance with a lot of modes over a broad bandwidth.
![20260625_060657_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20241104_20260628/20260625_060657_0.jpg)
![20260625_060657_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20241104_20260628/20260625_060657_1.jpg)
   - this looks so damn fun and painful at the same time. The pictured chip above supports 192 channels.
   - average coupling efficiency about -8 dB.
   - Jarczynski2006: [Integrated three-dimensional optical multilayer using free-space optics]( https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.45.006335)
