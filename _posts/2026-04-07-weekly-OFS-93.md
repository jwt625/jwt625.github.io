# Reflections on AI optimization and human values

man some of the speakers in this AI space really make anti-human cases and say things very out of sync of reality. 

I used to be an obsessed optimizer myself, I would think about what is the shortest route to where I was going all the time without realizing it, I would agree with my fellow optimizer friends that if you cause a traffic jam on the high way and collectively wasted so many man hour you deserve getting run over inside your car to clear up the traffic, and I would believe in social Darwinism and the more it filters the better the society. But over the years I've evolved from it, from the frustration that most people are not fierce optimizers, that I should just apply my optimization standard to myself. From the realization that humans only feel the contrast, and optimized results would not be the optimal had you not seen or feel the rest of the paths. To just enjoying the optimized and unoptimized world, taking the bus route that is just vaguely going towards the direction I wanted, and returing back to the 10 yr old me thats jumping in and out of dirty water puddle in the rain for no good reason, the me before all the life optimization trainings.

So this kind of speech is fine as well. Some people want to manufacture intelligenece with industrial efficiency and minimal total cost of ownership, some people want to grow intelligence with love and care, some people want to reduce the amount of intelligence. You may never be on each other's team, but you would not be you without the other ones. I do believe there are objectively good and evil acts towards an entity, maybe next time I'll yap on this.
