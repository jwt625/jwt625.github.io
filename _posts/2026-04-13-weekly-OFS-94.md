# SFQ control of superconducting qubits at millikelvin temperatures

I see more and more people using SFQ to control superconducting qubits now, and also moving to the same mK temperature, and reaching a single qubit gate fidelity of 99.9%, and 0.12 fJ per gate. kind of crazy there is no attenuators and filters etc. between the control chip and the qubit chip, and partly because of this the energy cost is ~1e4x better than room T control electronics.
![20250504_051323_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20250504_20260414/20250504_051323_0.jpg)
![20250504_051323_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20250504_20260414/20250504_051323_1.jpg)
   - Liu2026: [A plug-and-play superconducting quantum controller at millikelvin temperatures enables exceeding 99.9% average gate fidelity]( https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05693)
   - definitely very exciting, and should easily scale to thousands of qubits. Would need to show CZ next. Also not sure how much people have thought about calibrating and inverting the crosstalk matrix. wiring is still going to be an issue unless including digital serdes at one of the temperature stages.

# Photonic integrated circuits with metasurfaces for MOT

first time seeing a photonic integrated circuit chip combined with metasurfaces for a magneto-optical trap (MOT). (needed before loading the atoms into optical lattice for clocks and sensors and computers)

the optical loss is brutal over the whole system, and the waveguides show higher loss after exposed to high powers (~100 mW). All the nice aspects about highly confined photonic circuits might have to give way for support for higher power, and we'll see the return to planar lightwave circuits or equivalences (like really thin and weakly confined nitride).

# Quantum limit of coherent communication with squeezing

cool paper on the quantum limit of coherent communication with squeezing. but as all things quantum, you have to push all aspects to the classical limits first to make these quantum efforts worth it, like shot noise limited detection (instead of laser phase noise, DSP noise, fiber nonlinearity..), have low link budget so loss does not kill your squeezing (you can see they barely made the 3 dB needed), and in practice squeezing usually adds more noise along the unsqueezed axis, and you end up with fewer states in the phase space. Easier to go higher 2D constellation. practical aspect aside, you should read it to get the good math about coherent receivers plus the quantum considerations, how challenging it is to push to the quantum limited regime, and how they still did it.
![20260411_152203_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20250504_20260414/20260411_152203_0.jpg)
   - and it has a beautiful set of chips. SHG and SPDC for squeezing are not on-chip yet. 
   - Gurses2026: [Quantum coherent transceivers toward Holevo-limited communications]( https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07087) 
   - Would be a really sick system with amplifiers, SHG, SPDC all on chips and packaged together, and the parts already exist at different places.
