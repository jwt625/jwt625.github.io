# Alumina UV piezo-optomechanical modulators

alumina photonic integrated circuits made with ALD, plus AlN actuators, for UV applications. you probably know LN has 3.8 eV bandgap which is 326 nm, and SiN has 4.6 eV / 270 nm, so why is alumina needed? Defects. In practice you start getting significant absorption before reaching the bandgap energy. Alumina's bandgap is much larger (> 6 eV), and you can get sub 1 dB/cm at 360 nm with etched high confinement waveguide. I've made some piezo/MEMS photonics devices, and this kind of "inefficient" actuator designs piss me off. But I've also come to my resolution. It works, it is easier to make, and more reliable, and the application is not bottlenecked by energy consumption of the drive. So be it.
