# Dual-Band TFLN Modulator and Grating Coupler

another high bw TFLN EM modulator demo, but the more interesting design is this dual band grating coupler that sends C band one direction and O band the other direction, and a reflection based mux.
   - the sim looks like they have higher order modes along the thickness direction tho, 300 nm SiN + 300 nm LN is too thick.
![20260925_062643_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20260823_20260927/20260925_062643_0.jpg)
![20260925_062643_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20260823_20260927/20260925_062643_1.jpg)
   - Wang2026: [Dual-band silicon nitride-loaded lithium niobate electro-optic modulator with a record-breaking speed of 600 Gbps](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPTICA.609834)
   - microwave loss of the CL-TWE is 12 dB/cm at 100 GHz, they need to switch to thick BOx or quartz substrate.
   - TE1 slab mode will start appearing at ~470 nm of total thickness, 300+300 definitely too thick.
   - the GC's dual band directionality is better than id expect.
   ![20260925_063404_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20260823_20260927/20260925_063404_0.jpg)

# TFLN on 8-Inch SiPho from CUMEC

   - EO modulator has 3 dB bw around 100 GHz, PD around 50 GHz. The LN waveguide is so fat thus such long modulators. Vertical coupler IL is 0.1 dB. Edge coupler is pathetic 1.6 dB IL.

# Bandwidth vs Latency Scaling Rule
