# All-optical networking history and MEMS switches

maybe I should make a series from the previous wave of companies around the year 2000 and the numbers at the time. Here is some from Corvis Corp's S-1. They made all-optical networking equipments (gateways, amplifiers, add-drop multiplexers, and switches)
   - "Data traffic across communications networks is expected to increase from 350,000 terabytes per month in 1999 to more than 16 million terabytes per month in 2003, according to RHK, Inc., a leading market research and consulting firm. Ten terabytes is the equivalent of all of the information contained in the Library of Congress."
   - "In 1998 alone, service providers purchased $65 billion of electrical/optical transmission equipment and electrical switching equipment, according to RHK."
   - Lucent made MEMS based all-optical switches called LambdaRouter, and they were fighting over who made the first all-optical switch. LambdaRouter got terminated in August 2002, time goes by, and all-optical switching is reviving again since google's Apollo OCS.

# Laser cooling for computing and thermodynamics

in an alternative universe where energy is abundant, we'll have laser cooling for compute. The rack will be a big piece of complex crystal (or a blob of loose atoms idk) that is transparent, index matched, and AR coated around the cooling laser's and fluorescence wavelengths, and we will have a 100 megawatt laser beam to cool this 1 megawatt rack, in vacuum. It will be low entropy photons in, high entropy photons out, just like the whole earth.

# NAND memory high-aspect-ratio channel etching
