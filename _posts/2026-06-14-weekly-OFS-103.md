# Optical Time-Division Multiplexing

I've found the most optically annoying and the least serdes/dsp demanding optical networking architecture: optical serializer with a mode-locked laser (OTDM) so that you can have wide and slow signals coming in, and dual-comb-like homodyne detection with the same comb source as LO and same set of delays so that the detector and TIA bandwidths can also be small. You just need to hold path length drift/pulse overlap to be sub 10s of fs, and the optical phase, the branch skew, pulse shape..

# Photothermal Laser Direct Write

perception update again, TIL there is photothermal based laser direct write technique that can do ~5 nm feature size, works on metal and other phase change materials, and so far people have been making devices with narrow gaps for surface enhancement Raman scattering (SERS) with it
![20260207_192314_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240918_20260601/20260207_192314_0.jpg)
![20260207_192314_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240918_20260601/20260207_192314_1.jpg)
![20260207_192314_2.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240918_20260601/20260207_192314_2.jpg)
   - Qin2020: [5 nm Nanogap Electrodes and Arrays by Super-resolution Laser Lithography]( https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.nanolett.0c00978) 
   - Ti gap is made by converting to TiO2 and etch with HF+H2O2. Does not even require crazy fs lasers, it is just a few us laser pulse at 405 nm with 10-100 mW power. Claimed to be 10x faster than FIB. Could probably make it even faster with smarter optics.
   ![20260530_164015_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240918_20260601/20260530_164015_0.jpg)
   - bacause people are also making SNSPD with this technique, kind of perfect for making gaps out of metal. 
   - Huang2026: [Fabrication of superconducting nanowire arrays by nano laser direct writing]( https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0265844)
   ![20260530_164640_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240918_20260601/20260530_164640_0.jpg)
   ![20260530_164640_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240918_20260601/20260530_164640_1.jpg)
   - definitely at some point, but these are pretty long pulses, maybe those are just crappy crystal domains.
