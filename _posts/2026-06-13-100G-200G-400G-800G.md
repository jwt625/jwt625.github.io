These are photos of various pluggable transceivers I teardown over the last year or two, now finally gathered together properly.


# Intel

Red: Innolight 200G QSFP56 FR4 1310 nm optical transceiver.

Green: Intel 100G CWDM4 silicon photonics transceiver (2017).
- History of this legendary 100G SiPho transceivers TBA. It may be the first SiPho transceiver that has shipped multi millions of units, with intel's cursed InP on SOI process.
- By the way I have some of these transceiver 3D gaussian splatted. See

It really is like these. Can you find the isolator?
- these are EML based transceivers. You can see each one of them has a silicon lens, and then an array of polymer lens (?)
- then a prism for mux, and all goes thru a circulator that has a faraday rotator sandwiched between two polarizers
![20250122_043517_0.jpg](/assets/images/2025/20250117_20250127_long_thread/20250122_043517_0.jpg)
![VCSEL-MUX](/assets/images/2025/20250117_20250127_long_thread/VCSEL-MUX.png)
![VCSEL-MUX-transmission](/assets/images/2025/20250117_20250127_long_thread/VCSEL-MUX-transmission.png)
- the mux is done with multiple thin film filters and reflectors packed along the prism
- Lemoff2005: [Demonstration of a compact low-power 250-Gb/s parallel-WDM optical interconnect](https://doi.org/10.1109/LPT.2004.838157)
![20250122_043517_1.jpg](/assets/images/2025/20250117_20250127_long_thread/20250122_043517_1.jpg)
![20250122_043517_2.jpg](/assets/images/2025/20250117_20250127_long_thread/20250122_043517_2.jpg)
- color on the surface of the lens is likely AR coating
![20250122_043517_3.jpg](/assets/images/2025/20250117_20250127_long_thread/20250122_043517_3.jpg)
- the silicon (?) lens is soldered onto the carrier. On the TOSA you see a GSG high speed lane for the electro-absorptive modulator, as well as the DC bias with a capacitor for the laser / DFB.
- there is also a thermistor for temperature sensing. Or maybe it is just a simple resistor.

It is really similar to the kind you can find in some papers, like these CWDM8 transceiver using VCSELs, and datacenter WDM interconnect roadmap.
- Liu2023: [Lightwave Fabrics: At-Scale Optical Circuit Switching for Datacenter and Machine Learning Systems](https://doi.org/10.1145/3603269.3604836)

# Cisco Finisar

Cisco Finisar 400G DR4, converting 8x 50G to 4x 100G PAM4. I could see two EMLs, not sure where are the other two or they split them.

I got much better lighting for this one, and it is directly taken with iphone camera with a simple 2x magnification length, instead of thru the amscope.
