![20240807_053150_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20240807_053150_0.jpg)

- What kind of crazy ribbon wirebonds do you need to dump 50 A through this poor little thing...
- Saw it in [Laser Focus World's 2024 Innovators Awards](https://www.laserfocusworld.com/home/article/55130256/2024-innovators-awards-meet-the-honorees). Quite some other interesting stuff in there.

- The specified divergence is 55° × 10°, so you'll have to collimate it.

# Terabit DSL and THz Waveguides

Don't let them fool you when you hear DSL speed is ~tens of Mbps, and CAT6 speed is ~10 Gbps. Metal wires could guide THz waves, and people were dreaming and testing terabit DSL, using simple twisted pairs and such.

- Well, you might say these are so far sci-fi, but DSL has G.fast and MGfast (Multi-Gigabit fast) protocols that can do up to 2 Gbps and 10 Gbps (?!) over short reaches (<400 m and <100 m, respectively).

![20250223_070619_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_070619_0.jpg)
![20250223_070619_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_070619_1.jpg)

- Cioffi2018: [Terabit DSLs](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCOM.2018.1800597)
- Ahmed2025: [Estimation of channel capacity in CAT6 cable with circular waveguide modeling for future THz communication systems](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0233518)
- Here is a nice review on THz waveguides for communication: Sohoo2024, [Low Loss THz Waveguides and Its Potentials towards 6G Communication: A Brief Chronicle Review](https://www.ijeetc.com/show-240-1797-1.html)

![20250223_070946_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_070946_0.jpg)
![20250223_070946_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_070946_1.jpg)

- [G.fast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G.fast)
- Oksman2019: [MGFAST: A New Generation of Copper Broadband Access](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCOM.2019.1800844)
- Communication-system papers always have so many acronyms...

![20250223_071227_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_071227_0.jpg)

- A bit more on [MGfast](https://www.itu.int/hub/2021/08/up-to-8-gbit-s-broadband-with-new-itu-standard-mgfast/).
- The waveguide picture is from Markov2012, [Practical Metal-Wire THz Waveguides](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.2984). They only simulated the loss, and it's mostly dominated by bulk loss of the LDPE cladding, ~3 dB per meter.

![20250223_072302_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_072302_0.jpg)
![20250223_072302_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_072302_1.jpg)

- This is the OG metal-wire THz guiding work: Wang2004, [Metal wires for terahertz wave guiding](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature03040) ([open PDF](https://bpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/sites.brown.edu/dist/a/570/files/2024/07/waveguideNature.pdf)).
- The attraction of guiding with a bare wire is very low dispersion and propagation loss (it's even lower for higher frequencies??).
- However, it cannot bend much. When you see "can propagate on a slightly curved waveguide without a substantial increase in the loss," and no curvature is stated, it basically means don't do it.

![20250223_073111_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_073111_0.jpg)
![20250223_073111_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_073111_1.jpg)
![20250223_073111_2.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_073111_2.jpg)

- Man, I love this conclusion from the terabit DSLs paper.

![20250223_073830_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_073830_0.jpg)

- This thread is becoming a THz-wave fun thread.
- SLA 3D-printed WR15 and WR8 waveguides (20 µm layer height and 10 µm xy resolution), then "plated using electro-less copper (thickness around 4 µm) and a thin protective layer of gold (thickness around 0.1 µm)," with a frequency-doubling chip inside!
- Skaik2021: [125 GHz Frequency Doubler Using a Waveguide Cavity Produced by Stereolithography](https://doi.org/10.1109/TTHZ.2021.3131915) ([open PDF](https://pure-oai.bham.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/159301300/21_12_IEEE_T_TST_125GHz_doubler.pdf))

![20250223_080610_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_080610_0.jpg)
![20250223_080610_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20250223_080610_1.jpg)

- You'll see some of the weirdest setups in "silicon photonics," but at terahertz, because you can have waves in probes, coax, rectangular waveguides, microstrips, and silicon waveguides.
- There's a relatively recent review with a lot of pretty pictures: Koala2022, [Nanophotonics-inspired all-silicon waveguide platforms for terahertz integrated systems](https://doi.org/10.1515/nanoph-2021-0673).
- Did some work along this line quite a while ago, and hopefully it will see light soon.

# AI in Industrial Optimization and Simulation

- That's often a language problem. We need better language to properly describe the conditions and objectives.

# VCSEL Phase Change Photonic Memory

This post was originally gonna trash-talk the horrible 40 dB insertion loss, with 10 dB port-to-port variation on top of it (and another 5 dB of variation over 20 nm of bandwidth), of the optical readout of the phase-change-material-based memory bank. But they have a cool 2D-addressable VCSEL array flip-chip-packaged with the "memory" chip, although it costs too much energy to write a single bit.

![20260723_062417_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20260723_062417_0.jpg)
![20260723_062417_1.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240807_20260727/20260723_062417_1.jpg)

- Look at these poor transmission measurements, a pretty fundamental limit in many photonic-computing approaches. You need depth? What's your component insertion loss? 0.1 dB? You better not have 300 of them in a single row.
- Guo2026: [Integrated photonic computing-in-memory with 2D-addressable VCSELs arrays for phase change materials actuation](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.596616)
