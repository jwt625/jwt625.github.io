# GPU Interconnect Copper vs Optics

• Majority of GPU-GPU links <2m Cu today:
- • Scale-up fabric ~9x BW of scale-out network
- • ~5:1 copper vs optics
- • Cu used for cost and reliability
- • Cu limited by reach and density (connectors)

• Majority of GPU-GPU links will remain <50m
- • Min 50% of scale-up links must >2m
- • Need low-cost, reliable, power-efficient optics
- • Must hit targets:
- • 30m distance
- • Approaching copper-level reliability
- • <5pJ/bit (not including PHY)
- • 1Tbps/mm If optics can be applied to 100% scale-up, volume grows >20x over next 5 years
