# Intracavity SHG output coupling

first time seeing second harmonic generation inside a laser cavity, and turns out the idea is almost 60 years old. It is a nonlinear output coupler, and funny enough under ideal optimal laser operation with gain saturation, the SHG power is the same as usual max laser output power without SHG. i.e., the SHG "autotunes" to the optimal output coupler config, and it adapts to any pump power, where a usual laser you have to adjust the output mirror accordingly.
   - there is more nuance behind this statement, but it roughly makes sense, you want more output coupling when you get more gain from pumping harder, the optimal of which turns out to be linear wrt. intracavity intensity, and SHG loss is proportional to intensity. The SHG output is only weakly dependent of the material SHG strength when laser is well above threshold and low intracavity loss, so you can shove anything in as well as be a bit away from (quasi) phase matched.
   - sadly this TFLN demo is not near this condition at all, but they still get considerable SH power. Now what if they made the SHG resonant as well?
![20261030_004144_0.jpg](/assets/images/2026/20240927_20261104/20261030_004144_0.jpg)
   - seems like you do not win much from making the SH resonant..
   - i hate this kind of E-DBR... horrible tunable mirror, i can come up with a few different ways to tune a mirror much more effectively. I guess advantage is you are not as sensitive to the noise on the tuning voltage.
   - Chen2026: [Rubidium-Referenced Frequency-Doubled Pockels Laser with High-Speed Electro-Optic Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37279)
